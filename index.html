Questions

Where do the jobs come from?

The government's own tender websites, Find a Tender and Contracts Finder. Every public body in the UK has to post its contracts there.

What happens after 30 days?

If it's finding you work, carry on for £15 a month. If not, do nothing and the emails stop.

Do you bid for me?

No. We find the jobs and flag the catches. You decide what to go for and submit the bid yourself.

Is it only council work?

It covers councils, the NHS, schools, housing associations, universities and government departments, plus main contractors looking for trades.
