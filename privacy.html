Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

TenderTrade sends small building firms a weekly list of public-sector tenders. This page explains what we collect when you sign up, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We keep it short and we don't sell your details to anyone.

What we collect

When you sign up for the free trial, we collect what you type into the form:

  • your name and business name
  • your email address
  • your main trade, the area you work in and the biggest job you'd take on
  • any accreditations you tick, such as CHAS or Constructionline

We don't ask for card or bank details during the trial.

Why we use it

We use your details only to run the service you signed up for: to pick the tenders that suit your trade, area and job size, and to email you your list. We may also email you about your trial, such as when it's about to end. The legal basis is that you've asked us to provide this service.

We won't send you unrelated marketing, and we won't sell, rent or share your details with other businesses for their own use.

Who helps us run it

A few outside services handle data for us. They only use it to provide their service to us.

ServiceWhat it does
FormspreeReceives the signup form and passes your answers to us by email. Formspree is based in the United States, so your form answers are transferred there under its standard data protection terms.
GitHub PagesHosts this website. Like most web hosts, it may log your IP address for security.
Google FontsSupplies the fonts on this site. Your browser fetches them from Google, which sees your IP address.

If we add an email-sending or payment service later, we'll update this page before we start using it.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while you're on the trial or subscribed. If you stop, we delete them within 30 days, unless we need to keep a record for tax or legal reasons.

Cookies

This site doesn't set its own cookies or use tracking or advertising tools.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • send you a copy of the details we hold about you
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your details
  • stop using your details

To do any of these, or to stop your weekly list, reply to any email we've sent you and tell us what you'd like. We'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Where the tender information comes from

The tenders we send come from the UK government's Find a Tender and Contracts Finder services. They contain public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
